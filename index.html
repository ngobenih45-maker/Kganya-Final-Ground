Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge a joining fee?

No. Kganya Final Ground Funeral Services does not charge a joining fee.

What is the waiting period?

The standard waiting period is 6 months. Members transferring from another funeral cover with valid proof may qualify for no waiting period.

Do you offer family funeral cover?

Yes. We provide affordable funeral cover for individuals, families, churches and community groups.
